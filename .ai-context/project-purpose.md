# Project Purpose

The platform helps parents better understand their children's educational progress.

Schools generate large amounts of academic information that parents often struggle to interpret.

The platform converts raw educational data into:

* Clear insights
* Learning gap identification
* Academic trend analysis
* Actionable recommendations

The primary mission is:

"Help every parent understand where their child stands and how they can help."

Target users:

1. Parents
2. Teachers
3. School leadership

Success is measured by:

* Parent engagement
* Understanding of educational data
* Early identification of learning gaps
* Improvement in student outcomes
